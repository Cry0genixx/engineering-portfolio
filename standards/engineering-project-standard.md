# Engineering Project Standard

## 1. Problem Definition

Every project shall begin with a clearly defined engineering problem.

## 2. Requirements

Functional and technical requirements shall be documented before implementation.

## 3. System Architecture

The system shall be decomposed into relevant subsystems and interfaces.

## 4. Design

Relevant mechanical, electrical, software and control design decisions shall be documented.

## 5. Implementation

Implementation shall be traceable to documented requirements where practical.

## 6. Verification

Each important requirement shall have a defined verification method.

## 7. Results

Results, limitations and deviations shall be documented.

## 8. Lessons Learned

Each completed project shall include a retrospective describing:

- What worked
- What did not work
- What was learned
- What would be changed in Revision 2

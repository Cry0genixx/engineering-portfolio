# Portfolio Mission

**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-04

## Purpose

The purpose of this engineering portfolio is to provide verifiable evidence of practical and technical competence across mechanical engineering, manufacturing, mechatronics, industrial automation and systems integration.

The portfolio shall demonstrate engineering capability through structured projects rather than relying solely on educational credentials, job titles, course certificates or self-reported skills.

The primary objective is to show the ability to take an industrial or technical problem from initial definition through design, implementation, integration, verification and documentation.

---

## Engineering Profile

The portfolio builds on an existing foundation in:

* CNC Machining and manufacturing
* G-code, machining processes and toolpaths
* Mechanical design and CAD
* Production engineering and optimization
* Tolerances, quality and manufacturability
* Practical industrial troubleshooting

This foundation shall be expanded and documented through projects involving:

* Industrial automation
* PLC programming
* HMI and operator interfaces
* Robotics
* Electrical systems
* Sensors and actuators
* Pneumatics and hydraulics
* Industrial communication
* IT/OT integration
* SCADA and production data
* Electronics and PCB design
* Systems engineering
* Verification and validation

---

## Portfolio Objective

The portfolio shall demonstrate the ability to:

1. Define technical and industrial problems clearly.
2. Translate problems into functional and technical requirements.
3. Develop suitable system architectures.
4. Design mechanical, electrical and control-system solutions.
5. Select and integrate appropriate technologies and components.
6. Implement structured and maintainable control software.
7. Integrate machines, robots, sensors, networks and higher-level systems.
8. Verify solutions against defined requirements.
9. Identify limitations, failure modes and design risks.
10. Produce professional and traceable engineering documentation.

---

## Portfolio Philosophy

Projects shall prioritize engineering depth over quantity.

A small number of well-documented projects demonstrating complete engineering workflows are considered more valuable than a large collection of isolated exercises.

Projects should, where practical, demonstrate the following workflow:
> Problem/
> Requirements/
> Architecture/
> Design/
> Implementation/
> Integration/
> Verification/
> Results/
> Lessons learned

Simulation and virtual commissioning are valid engineering methods where physical implementation is impractical, provided the limitations of the simulation are clearly stated.

No project shall imply physical implementation, industrial deployment or verification that has not actually taken place.

---

## Evidence Principle

A skill is not considered strongly demonstrated merely because it appears on a CV, LinkedIn profile or course certificate.

Strong portfolio evidence should include one or more of the following:

* Engineering calculations
* CAD models
* Technical drawings
* Electrical schematics
* PLC or software source code
* State diagrams
* System architecture diagrams
* I/O documentation
* Network architecture
* Simulation results
* Test specifications
* Test results
* Data analysis
* Videos or screenshots of operation
* Design decisions and trade-off analysis
* Failure analysis
* Lessons learned

Where possible, evidence shall be traceable to a specific project and requirement.

## Target Capability

The long-term goal is to demonstrate a multidisciplinary engineering profile capable of working across the boundaries between:
> Manufacturing/
> Mechanical Engineering/
> Mechatronics/
> Automation/
> Robotics/
> Industrial IT/OT/
> Systems Engineering

The portfolio is intended to support applications and technical discussions related to roles such as:

* Mechatronics Engineer
* Automation Engineer
* Robotics Engineer
* Manufacturing Engineer
* Production Engineer
* Systems Engineer
* Industrialization Engineer
* Controls Engineer
* Technical Project Engineer
* Industrial Systems Engineer

The portfolio is not intended to claim equivalence to any specific academic degree.

Instead, it shall provide concrete evidence of actual engineering capability and allow the techincal work to be evaluated on its own merits.

---

## Quality Standard

Public portfolio projects should meet the following minimum standard:

* Clear technical objective
* Defined scope
* Documented requirements
* Understandable architecture
* Traceable implementation
* Verification against requirements
* Honest description of limitations
* Professional documentation
* Reproducable or understandable project structure

Projects may remain private or in development until they meet this standard.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The portfolio should evolve into a structured engineering evidence base containing:

* Techincal laboratories
* Multidisciplinary engineering projects
* Major flagship projects
* Reusable engineering templates
* Techincal documentation
* Engineering decision records
* Verified competency evidence

Over time, the portfolio should demonstrate not only the ability to use engineering tools, but the ability to apply them systematically to solve realistic industrial problems.
